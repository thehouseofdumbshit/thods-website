The people

Not a directory.
A discovery.

The House does not operate a public Artist marketplace or accept open account registrations. Artists are introduced through existing relationships and referrals, then considered by the Head of Patronage & Commissions. Approved Artists are presented privately by Custodians in the context of a particular commission.
